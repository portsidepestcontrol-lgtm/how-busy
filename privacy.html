# How Busy? Privacy Policy **Last updated: 13 October 2026** How Busy? respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and managed when you use the How Busy? app. ## 1. Information we may collect Depending on how you use the app, How Busy? may process: - **Location information:** location data used to identify nearby venues and support venue entry and exit detection. - **Venue presence information:** information used to estimate how many app users are present at a venue. - **Profile information:** information you choose to provide, such as profile preferences or whether you identify as single or taken. - **Crowd reports:** information you voluntarily submit about venue crowds. - **Subscription information:** premium subscription status handled through Apple or Google’s payment systems. The app’s actual collection depends on the features you use and your device permissions. ## 2. How information is used Information may be used to provide nearby venue listings, estimate live venue activity, display crowd trends, support profile features, operate premium features, maintain app security and troubleshoot problems. ## 3. Location information How Busy? uses location permissions to help identify nearby venues and support automatic venue presence detection. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may affect location-dependent features. ## 4. Storage and service providers How Busy? uses third-party technology and service providers, which may include Supabase, to support app data and functionality. Information processed by these providers is subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices. ## 5. Subscriptions and payments Premium subscriptions are offered through the applicable Apple App Store or Google Play billing system. How Busy? does not ask you to provide your full payment card details directly to the app. Subscription management and payment processing are handled by the relevant platform. ## 6. Sharing information Information may be processed by service providers to operate the app. How Busy? does not claim to sell your personal information. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. ## 7. Security and retention Reasonable steps are taken to protect information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained only as needed for app functionality, legitimate operational purposes and legal obligations. ## 8. Your choices You can manage location permissions through your device settings. You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with you or request assistance with privacy concerns. ## 9. Age and responsible use How Busy? is intended for adults aged 18 and over. The app provides venue information and is not an endorsement of excessive alcohol consumption. Please drink responsibly and follow local laws. ## 10. Changes to this policy This policy may be updated as the app changes. The latest version will be published at this page. ## 11.
