Frequently asked questions

Why can't the app detect my venue?

Check that Location Services are enabled for How Busy? in your iPhone Settings. Allow location access and use “Refresh My Location” if available. Detection can depend on GPS accuracy and your distance from the venue.

Why does a venue show zero people?

Live activity depends on participating devices and available reports. A zero or missing count may mean there is not enough recent activity data; it does not necessarily mean the venue is empty.

How do I update my crowd profile?

Open the Profile section in the app and update the available profile selections. Please refer to the app's privacy information for details about profile data.

How does Premium work?

Premium features may include aggregated venue intelligence, crowd balance, singles insights and historical trends when sufficient data is available. Subscription billing is handled through the relevant app store.

How do I manage or cancel an Apple subscription?

On iPhone, open Settings, tap your Apple Account name, choose Subscriptions, select How Busy? if it appears, and follow the available options. Apple controls billing and cancellation for App Store subscriptions.

How do I report a problem?

Email howbusy26@gmail.com and describe the issue. Do not include passwords, payment card details or other sensitive information.
