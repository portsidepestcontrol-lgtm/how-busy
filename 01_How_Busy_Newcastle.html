📍 How Busy? uses location

How Busy? uses your phone's location to work out which venue you are at and help create live crowd information.

What we use
  • Your phone's location to detect a nearby venue.
  • An anonymous installation ID to count an active presence.
  • Your saved How Busy? answers, such as single/taken and your crowd answer, where you have provided them.
What we don't need
  • We don't need to store your name to count venue presence.
  • Exact GPS coordinates are matched on your phone and are not stored in the live presence record.
  • We don't sell your location data.

Location must be allowed by your phone's operating system. On iPhone and Android, you can change this permission later in Settings.
